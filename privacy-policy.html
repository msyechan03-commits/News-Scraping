Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 28, 2026

Introduction

News Brief Bot ("we", "our", "us") operates a WhatsApp-based automated news briefing service. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information.

Information We Collect

We collect only the minimum information necessary to deliver our service:

- WhatsApp phone numbers of registered recipients for message delivery purposes only.

- No personal data, browsing history, or location data is collected.

How We Use Information

Phone numbers are used solely to deliver automated daily economic news briefings via WhatsApp Business API. We do not sell, share, or distribute any user information to third parties.

Data Storage and Security

Recipient phone numbers are stored securely as encrypted environment variables. We do not maintain a user database. All message delivery is handled through Meta's WhatsApp Business Cloud API infrastructure.

Third-Party Services

We use Meta's WhatsApp Business Cloud API to deliver messages. Meta's own privacy policy governs their handling of message delivery data.

Data Retention

We do not retain message content or delivery logs beyond the immediate delivery process.
